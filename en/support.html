Capsort Support

Capsort is an app for sorting the screenshots you take, and links received from the share sheet, into categories you create.

Contact

To report a problem or ask a question, please open an issue on GitHub. English and Japanese are both welcome.
https://github.com/yktssss9-blip/capsort-site/issues

It helps if you include the following. Please do not post photos or personal information in this public space.

  • Your iPhone / iPad model and iOS version
  • Your Capsort version
  • What happened, and what you did just before

FAQ

I can’t sort right after taking a screenshot

You need to allow full access to your Photos library and add the shortcut. You can go through the steps again from “Shortcut Setup Guide” in Settings. The shortcut cannot launch the App from the Lock Screen.

My categories don’t show up in the Photos app

Even if reflecting changes in the Photos app fails, your sorting is saved in the App, and it is retried automatically the next time you open the App. You can check the status under “Waiting to Sync” in Settings.

Capsort doesn’t appear in the share sheet

Scroll the top row of the share sheet to the end, tap “More” (or “Edit”), and add Capsort. You can review the steps under “Sort from Share” in Settings.

The title or thumbnail of a link doesn’t appear

Titles and thumbnails are fetched from the linked website over the network. Depending on your connection or the website, they may not be available. You can still sort the link.

What happens to my photos if I delete the App?

The images in your Photos library and the albums in the Photos app remain. Your categories, sorting records, and saved links in Capsort are deleted.

I bought Capsort Pro on another device

Capsort Pro is a one-time purchase. Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap “Restore Purchases” on the Capsort Pro screen.
